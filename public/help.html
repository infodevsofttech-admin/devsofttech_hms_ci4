02Hospital Roles & Permission Structure

E-Atria HMIS enforces strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). What each user sees in the sidebar and which action buttons are clickable depend on their assigned roles and permissions:

Role / Staff Area Primary HMS Responsibilities Accessible Menu Modules Restricted Boundaries
Front Desk / Reception Patient search, registration, ABHA creation/verification, OPD visit tokens, bed inquiries, basic receipt printing. Patient List, OPD Invoice, Bed Status, ABDM OPD Queue Cannot prescribe medications, sign diagnostic reports, or execute financial ledgers.
Doctor / Clinician Consultations, vitals, clinical examination, e-Prescribing, investigations ordering, OT requests, discharge summaries. OPD Appointment List, Rx-Group, Clinical Templates, Doctor Documents, OT Queue, Discharge Cannot collect cash, modify billing tariffs, or approve pharmacy stock indents.
Nursing Staff Bedside care, vitals charting, medication administration, OT prep, nursing notes, pre-op safety checks. IPD Patient List, OT Surgery Queue, Doctor Documents Cannot discharge patient without doctor summary or edit finalized bills.
Pathologist / Radiologist Sample tracking, parameter entry, imaging study review, findings drafting, digital signature. Diagnosis (Pathology, X-Ray, CT, MRI, USG, Echo), Diagnosis Report Cannot modify verified reports without audited edit reason and permission.
Pharmacist Prescription dispensing, batch/expiry selection, counter sales, returns, stock indents, supplier receipts. Pharmacy (Medical Store), Pharmacy Reports, Hospital Stock Cannot approve financial payouts or modify OPD consultation fees.
Billing Cashier Diagnostic invoicing, bed charge billing, advance deposits, Ayushman/TPA claims, shift cash closure. Charges Invoice, IPD Billing, Org Packing, Cash Submission Cannot accept their own cash submission or edit doctor clinical notes.
Accountant / Finance Cashbook verification, shift acceptance, bank deposits, statement reconciliation, doctor consult payouts. Accounts And Finance, Payment Edit, Bank Audit, Payouts Cannot alter medical diagnosis or delete clinical encounter history.
Administrator User accounts, role permissions, tariff masters, template configuration, hospital settings, system audit. All Menus, Admin Panel, Template Workspace, Charges Master Clinical actions still record the administrator's individual audit footprint.
Access Denied Error: If an "Access Denied: Missing Permission" modal appears, note the exact module name and notify the Hospital System Administrator to review the user's role in Admin → User Management.

03Patient Management & Registration Module

Sidebar Menu: Billing → Patient List
Intended Users: Front Desk Receptionists, OPD Registration Staff, Admission Clerks.

This module serves as the primary master registry for all individuals receiving healthcare services at the facility. It provides comprehensive duplicate prevention, ABDM ABHA creation and verification, quick search, and direct launching of OPD, IPD, and Emergency encounters.

Screen 1: Search Tab

Purpose: Locate existing hospital patients within seconds and prevent duplicate file generation.

  • Search by Patient Code (UHID) (e.g. P26091000018).
  • Search by 10-Digit Mobile Number.
  • Search by Patient Full Name (supports partial matching, e.g. "Rahul").
  • Search by Aadhaar Number (12 digits) or 14-Digit ABHA ID.
  • Search by ABHA Address Handle (e.g. rahul@sbx).
  • Action: Clicking on any patient row opens their comprehensive Patient 360° Profile & Medical History.

Screen 2: New Person Registration Form

Purpose: Register a brand-new patient into the hospital master database. All fields are explained below:

Optional		 Government unique identity number. Used for duplicate checking across the database and mandatory for Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY) claims and official schemes.

Auto-filled via ABHA		 Ayushman Bharat Health Account national identifier. Prevents duplicate patient creation and links hospital files to national health ecosystem.

Mandatory *		 Primary communication mobile number. Used for SMS appointment alerts, OTP verification, billing invoice delivery, and ABDM discovery matching.
Title & Full Name Salutation Dropdown & Text
Mandatory *		 Patient's legal name. Appears on all official medical records, prescriptions, diagnostic reports, and medico-legal documents.

Mandatory *		 Clinical gender. Critical for ward bed allocation, diagnostic reference range calculations (e.g. Hemoglobin), and obstetrics/gynecology workflows.

Screen 3: ABHA Management Tab

Purpose: Seamless national Ayushman Bharat digital health identity integration directly at the registration desk:

1. Create / Find ABHA Record
Assisted ABHA creation via Aadhaar OTP. Includes the required Healthcare Worker Declaration and Beneficiary Consent Name Input before sending OTP. Creates ABHA number and address handle in 30 seconds.
2. Verify / Find ABHA Account
Verify existing ABHA profiles via 14-digit number, @sbx handle, or registered mobile number. Retrieves verified demographic data and photo from ABDM.
3. Scan ABHA QR
Uses handheld 2D barcode scanner or camera to read physical ABHA card or mobile PHR app QR code, populating demographics with zero manual typing.
4. Link Records to ABHA (HIP)
Opens the M2 HIP Linking modal. Links patient visits directly to ABHA using Gateway Method 4 (Demographic Auth) or sends a Deep Link SMS to the patient's smartphone.

Screen 4: Patient 360° Profile Hub

Once a patient is registered, their profile displays emergency and clinical shortcuts:

  • Appointment For OPD — Generates OPD visit slip and assigns consulting doctor.
  • Add Charge — Directly books diagnostic tests (Pathology, X-Ray, CT, MRI, USG) or procedures.
  • Casualty — Emergency admission under ESI triage.
  • Day Care — Short-stay procedure admission (<24 hours).
  • IPD — Standard inpatient ward bed admission.
  • Link Records to ABHA (HIP) — Initiates instant care context linking.

04Outpatient Department (OPD) Workflow

Sidebar Menus: Billing → OPD Invoice | Doctor's Work → OPD Appointment List

OPD Check-In & Token Generation

  1. Reception Registration: Receptionist opens patient profile and clicks Appointment For OPD.
  2. Consultation Parameters: Select Department (General Medicine, Orthopedics, Cardiology, etc.), Doctor, Fee Category (Normal, Revisit, Free, Camp), and Organization/Insurance case if applicable.
  3. Receipt & Token: System generates OPD Slip with unique visit token (e.g. Token #14) and care context reference OPD-{PatientId}-S{OpdId}-{Date}.
  4. ABDM OPD Queue: Patients scanning the hospital facility QR code via ABHA app appear automatically in the ABDM OPD Queue under Front Desk with pre-filled demographics for 1-click token creation.

Doctor OPD Consultation & Prescription Desk

Doctors access the consultation workspace from their daily appointment list. The screen contains dedicated clinical sections:

  • Vitals & Physical Examination: BP (Systolic/Diastolic), Pulse, SpO2, Temperature, Respiratory Rate, Height, Weight, BMI, Waist circumference, and general examination tags (Pallor, Icterus, Cyanosis, Clubbing, Edema).
  • Chief Complaints: Multi-select complaint box integrated with standard SNOMED-CT terminology. Allows entering duration and severity.
  • Diagnosis: Provisional and Final diagnosis search powered by ICD-10 and SNOMED-CT clinical coding.
  • Prescription (Medicines): Search by brand or generic name. Specify dosage (e.g. 500mg), frequency (e.g. 1-0-1, TID), timing (After food, Empty stomach), route (Oral, IV, IM), and duration in days. Automated drug interaction alerts activate if duplicate molecules are prescribed.
  • Investigation Orders: Prescribe pathology tests or radiology imaging with 1 click. Automatically creates unbilled investigation requests at the diagnostic billing desk.
  • Advice & Precautions: Standard diet, lifestyle, and recovery instructions. Supports inserting pre-defined advice snippets.
  • Next Visit / Follow-up: Set scheduled review date (e.g. "After 7 Days" or specific calendar date).
  • ABDM Push: When saving, clicking Share to ABHA automatically generates the HL7 FHIR R4 OPConsultRecord bundle and makes it available to the patient's ABHA app.

05In-Patient (IPD), Day Care & Emergency (Casualty)

Sidebar Menus: In-Patient & Nursing Care → IPD Patient List | Billing → IPD Billing
Compliant Standards: NABH 5th Edition, ESI Triage Levels 1–5, PADSS Criteria, HL7 FHIR R4.

1. Admission Categories & Key Identifiers

Encounter Type Identifier Prefix Designated Ward Clinical Purpose
Emergency / Casualty ER-YYYY-XXXXX Casualty & Emergency Ward (ER-01) Acute trauma, chest pain, stroke, resuscitation, and medico-legal admissions.
Day Care (<24 Hrs) DC-YYYY-XXXXX Day Care Unit (DC-01) Planned surgical/medical procedures (Cataract, Hemodialysis, Chemotherapy, Endoscopy).
Inpatient (IPD) A-YYYY-XXXXX General / Semi-Private / ICU / HDU Multi-day inpatient admissions requiring continuous hospital stay.

2. Emergency / Casualty Admission & ESI Triage

Casualty staff immediately triage arriving patients into 5 international Emergency Severity Index (ESI) tiers:

  • ESI 1 (Resuscitation) — Immediate life-threat (cardiac arrest, severe shock). 0 min door-to-doctor.
  • ESI 2 (Emergent) — High risk, danger vitals, acute chest pain. ≤10 min door-to-doctor.
  • ESI 3 (Urgent) — Stable vitals needing multiple resources (IV fluids, Labs, X-Ray). ≤30 min.
  • ESI 4 (Less Urgent) — 1 resource needed (simple suture, uncomplicated UTI). ≤60 min.
  • ESI 5 (Non-Urgent) — Medication refill, minor rash. ≤120 min.
Medico-Legal Case (MLC): For road accidents, poisoning, burns, or assault, toggle Is MLC. Enter Police Station, Constable Badge, and Brought-By details for official intimation.

3. Clinical Escalation: Convert to IPD with SBAR Handover

When a Casualty or Day Care patient deteriorates or requires prolonged admission, click "Convert to IPD". The system enforces the SBAR clinical handover protocol:

SSituation: Immediate clinical justification for inpatient conversion.
BBackground: Baseline clinical history, co-morbidities, presenting symptoms.
AAssessment: Current vital signs, Glasgow Coma Scale (GCS), stat lab results, emergency medications given.
RRecommendation: Target inpatient ward, ICU monitoring orders, scheduled specialist consults.
Unified Billing Guarantee: Converting to IPD automatically transfers all prior emergency or daycare charges into the unified Inpatient Bill under clearly demarcated sub-headers (EMERGENCY CHARGES, DAY CARE CHARGES, ROOM CHARGES). No charges are lost.

4. Day Care Discharge & PADSS Scoring

Before discharge from Day Care, patients must be evaluated against the Post-Anaesthetic Discharge Scoring System (PADSS) across 5 clinical parameters (Vital signs, Ambulation, Nausea/vomiting, Pain control, Surgical bleeding). A score of ≥ 9 out of 10 is mandatory for home discharge.

06Operation Theatre (OT) Management

Sidebar Menu: In-Patient & Nursing Care → OT Surgery Queue

  1. Surgery Request: Treating surgeon submits surgery request specifying procedure name, operating surgeon, assistant, anaesthetist, required OT room, priority (Elective / Emergency), and requested date/time.
  2. Pre-Operative Assessment: Pre-anaesthetic checkup (PAC) recording airway grading (Mallampati), ASA physical status score (I–V), blood cross-match confirmation, and NPO status.
  3. OT Scheduling: OT supervisor allocates OT Table and timing, checking for equipment or instrument tray conflicts.
  4. Patient Call: Ward nurse updates patient status to Called to OT and performs pre-op surgical checklist (consent signed, jewellery removed, dentures removed, surgical site marked).
  5. WHO Surgical Safety Checklist Gates:
    • Sign-In (Before Induction): Confirm patient identity, procedure, site marking, pulse oximeter attached, allergy checks, difficult airway risk, anticipated blood loss.
    • Time-Out (Before Skin Incision): Entire team introduces themselves; surgeon, anaesthetist, and nurse confirm patient name, procedure, antibiotic prophylaxis given ≤60 min prior, essential imaging displayed.
    • Sign-Out (Before Patient Leaves OT): Nurse verbally confirms instrument and sponge counts, specimen labeling, equipment malfunctions, post-op recovery plan.
  6. Operative & Anaesthesia Notes: Surgeon logs operative findings, implants used (with serial/lot numbers for tracking), sutures, drainage tubes, and post-op recovery orders.

07Doctor's Work & Clinical Masters

Sidebar Menu: Doctor's Work (Appointments, Rx-Group, OPD Medicine, Clinical Masters, Clinical Templates, Document Issue, Immunization)

1. Rx-Group Master (Prescription Protocol Sets)

Purpose: Build reusable multi-medicine prescription sets for 1-click prescribing during busy OPD hours.

  • Create groups such as "Acute Gastroenteritis Kit", "Hypertension Starter", or "Viral Fever Pack".
  • Each group contains pre-configured dosages, frequencies, and instructions. During consultation, picking one group populates all medicines instantly.

2. Clinical Master Workspace (Complaints, Profiles & Advice)

Purpose: Maintain hospital-wide clinical dictionaries:

  • Chief Complaints Master: Frequently presenting symptoms mapped to standard clinical concepts.
  • Investigation Master: Master catalog of diagnostic tests and individual laboratory parameters.
  • Investigation Profile Master: Grouping individual tests into test batteries (e.g. Lipid Profile containing Total Cholesterol, Triglycerides, HDL, LDL, VLDL).
  • OPD Advice Master: Standard dietary, physical therapy, and medication precautions.

3. Doctor Document & Certificate Issuance Desk

Purpose: Issue legal and clinical certificates directly from the patient encounter:

  • Medical Leave & Fitness Certificates
  • Referral Slips to higher tertiary centers
  • Birth Intimation and Death Audit Slips
  • Emergency Travel Medical Clearance

4. Pediatric Immunization Tracker

Purpose: Track national vaccine schedules for infants and children:

  • Pre-configured national immunization schedule (BCG, OPV, Hepatitis B, Pentavalent, Rotavirus, Measles, MMR).
  • Record administered date, batch number, manufacturer, and injection site.
  • System automatically schedules and alerts next due dates.

08Diagnosis, Laboratory & Imaging Modules

Sidebar Menu: Diagnosis (Pathology, Biopsy, X-Ray, MRI, CT-Scan, Ultrasound, Echo)

1. Pathology & Biopsy Workflow

  1. Sample Collection & Accession: Phlebotomist collects sample, assigns unique Lab Accession Number / Barcode, and records collection time.

  • Radiologist Sign-off: Consultant radiologist enters clinical findings and final impression, then signs off.
  • ABDM DiagnosticReportRecord Push: Pushes verified imaging reports to ABDM Gateway for immediate viewing in the patient's ABHA app.

09Template Design & Configuration Guide

Sidebar Menu: Admin & Settings → Template
Intended Users: Radiologists, Pathologists, Chief Medical Officers, Hospital Administrators.

Standardized templates ensure clinical precision, reduce reporting turnaround time by over 70%, and ensure medico-legal compliance across all imaging and diagnostic disciplines.

1. Diagnostic Modality Navigation & Scope

In E-Atria HMIS, pre-configured diagnostic report templates are organized into dedicated modality tabs under Admin & Settings → Template:

Diagnostic Modality How to Access in System Clinical Scope & Typical Investigations
Ultrasound (USG) Click Admin & Settings → Template, then select the Ultrasound tab. USG Whole Abdomen, Pelvis, KUB, Obstetric / Antenatal Scans, Thyroid, Scrotum, Venous & Arterial Doppler.
Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) Click Admin & Settings → Template, then select the MRI tab. MRI Brain (plain & contrast), Cervical/Lumbar Spine, Knee / Shoulder Joints, MRCP, Whole Body MRI.
Digital X-Ray (Radiography) Click Admin & Settings → Template, then select the X-Ray tab. Plain & Contrast Radiography: Chest PA View, KUB, Extremities (Bones & Joints), Spine AP/Lateral views, Barium studies.
Computed Tomography (CT-Scan) Click Admin & Settings → Template, then select the CT-Scan tab. NCCT Head, HRCT Thorax, CECT Abdomen & Pelvis, CT Angiography, PNS (Coronal/Axial views).
2D Echocardiography (Echo) & Special Click Admin & Settings → Template, then select the Echocardiography / Special tab. Transthoracic 2D Echo, Color Doppler, Left Ventricular Ejection Fraction (LVEF), Valvular regurgitation assessment.

2. How to Design a New Radiology Template (Step-by-Step)

  1. Open Modality Template Workspace: Go to Admin & Settings → Template and click on the desired modality tab (e.g. X-Ray, CT-Scan, MRI, or Ultrasound).
  2. Click "+ Add Template": Opens the rich-text template builder.
  3. Specify Template Title: Enter a clear, standardized name (e.g. X-RAY CHEST PA VIEW - NORMAL STUDY or CT HEAD - NORMAL BRAIN STUDY).
  4. Link Hospital Service / Billing Test:
    Automatic Template Loading: In the "Linked Charge / Test Item" dropdown, select the corresponding test name from the Hospital Charges Master. When reception bills this service on an OPD or IPD slip, the system will automatically inject this template into the doctor's report editor without requiring manual search!
  5. Structure the Clinical Content: Adhere to professional NABH reporting standards by dividing the template into 4 standard sections:
    Recommended Clinical Template Anatomy
    CLINICAL INDICATION:
Suspected chest pathology / Routine pre-operative medical evaluation.

TECHNIQUE:
Standard plain radiograph of the chest was obtained in the postero-anterior (PA) erect position at full inspiration.

FINDINGS:
• Trachea is central in position.
• Cardiothoracic ratio is within normal limits. Cardiac size, shape, and contours appear normal.
• Both lung fields are clear with normal vascularity. No focal parenchymal consolidation, infiltrate, mass, or cavitation detected.
• Both costophrenic and cardiophrenic angles are sharp and clear. No pleural effusion seen.
• Mediastinal and hilar contours appear normal with no lymphadenopathy.
• The visualised thoracic cage, ribs, clavicles, and soft tissues appear unremarkable.
• Both diaphragmatic domes are smooth and normal in position.

IMPRESSION:
NO SIGNIFICANT ABNORMALITY DETECTED ON CHEST RADIOGRAPH.
NORMAL STUDY.
  6. Save & Test Template: Click Save Template. The template is immediately active across all diagnostic report editors.

3. Pathology Parameter & Reference Interval Design

Pathology templates configure individual analytical parameters:

  • Parameter Name: e.g. Fasting Blood Glucose, Serum Creatinine, Platelet Count.
  • Unit of Measure: e.g. mg/dL, g/dL, /cumm, mmol/L.
  • Reference Ranges: Age and gender specific normal minimum and maximum thresholds. Readings outside these limits automatically flag as LOW or HIGH in red text.

  3. Cashier enters physical currency breakdown (e.g. 500x10, 200x5, 100x20).
  4. Click Submit Cash Statement to lock the shift and handover cash to the Accounts Department.

15ABDM (Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission) Ecosystem

Sidebar Menu: ABDM (ABDM Task Board, ABHA Patient Request List, ABDM OPD Queue, ABDM Bridge Log)
Compliant Standard: ABDM HIECM V3 Standard, FHIR R4 Bundle Specifications.

1. Milestone 1 (M1): ABHA Creation & Verification

  • Aadhaar OTP Creation: Enter 12-digit Aadhaar → healthcare worker and beneficiary declarations are collected → OTP verified → ABHA Number & Address generated.
  • ABHA Verification: Retrieve verified demographic profile and photograph from ABDM via mobile OTP or ABHA number.
  • Facility Scan & Share: Generates hospital QR code displayed at reception. Patients scan QR via their ABHA app, populating their details directly in the ABDM OPD Queue for 1-click token creation.

2. Milestone 2 (M2): Health Information Provider (HIP) Linking

HIP-Initiated Linking (Method 4)
Hospital submits patient demographics to ABDM Gateway. Gateway returns a secure Link Token to link hospital care contexts directly.
Deep Link SMS Notification
Hospital dispatches an SMS containing a direct app link to the patient's smartphone for 1-tap care context linking in their ABHA app.
User-Initiated Discovery
Patient searches hospital in their PHR app using mobile number → HMS dynamically returns matching care contexts → verified with mobile OTP.

3. Milestone 2 (M2): Health Information Exchange (FHIR R4 Data Transfer)

When the patient requests their health records in their ABHA app, the HMS generates and transfers encrypted FHIR R4 bundles:

Clinical Document Type FHIR Resource Type Care Context Reference Clinical Content Transferred
OPD Consultation OPConsultRecord Unique Visit Receipt Number Encounter, Vitals, Chief complaints, ICD-10 diagnosis, prescribed medicines with dosage, doctor advice.
Diagnostic & Radiology DiagnosticReportRecord Diagnostic Lab Requisition ID Laboratory values, reference ranges, radiologist imaging findings, impression, and PDF study attachments.
Discharge Summary DischargeSummaryRecord Inpatient Admission Number Admission/discharge dates, clinical course, procedures performed, discharge medications, follow-up advice.

4. Milestone 3 (M3): Health Information User (HIU)

Allows hospital doctors to request digital consent from arriving patients to view their previous medical history and diagnostic reports from other hospitals across India via the ABHA Patient Request List (Digital Consent Management).

17Admin, System Settings & Master Configuration

Sidebar Menu: Admin & Settings (Admin Panel, Template, Charges Master, Bed Status)

1. Bed Status & Ward Configuration (`setting/admin/bed-status`)

  • Configure hospital physical departments, wards (e.g. General, ICU, NICU, Day Care, Casualty), and rooms.
  • Define bed numbers, daily per-diem room tariff rates, and room amenities (Oxygen, Ventilator, AC).
  • Live real-time graphical bed census showing Occupied, Vacant, Cleaning, and Maintenance beds.

2. User Management & Access Permissions

  • Create system users with individual usernames and secure passwords.
  • Assign users to system groups (Doctor, Reception, Nursing, Accounts, Pharmacy, Admin).

18Troubleshooting Common Operational Issues

Observed Issue Root Cause Recommended Action & Resolution
"Access Denied: Missing Permission" User account does not possess the required RBAC privilege. Ask Administrator to open Admin → User Management → Permissions and assign the required permission flag.
"Incorrect OTP" during ABHA Entered OTP digits do not match UIDAI or ABDM Gateway token. Re-verify the 6-digit OTP on patient's mobile handset. Ensure OTP has not expired (>10 min).
Duplicate Identifier Alert Aadhaar number or ABHA ID is already linked to an existing patient file. Click the prompt's "View Existing Patient Profile" link to open the already registered record.
Care Context Not Appearing in ABHA App Patient searched in PHR app using a mobile number different from hospital record. Verify that the patient's mobile number registered in hospital records matches the mobile registered on patient's ABHA account.
PDF Print Preview Blank or Misaligned Browser popup blocker active, or paper size settings mismatch. Allow popups for the HMS domain. In printer dialogue, ensure Paper Size is set to A4 with Margins set to Default.
Wrong Medicine Batch in POS Expired or depleted batch selected manually. Ensure FEFO suggestion is accepted. Perform stock reconciliation in Pharmacy → Medical Store.
Technical Support Escalation: When reporting an unresolved issue to technical support, include:
  1. The active module/screen name and patient UHID (or invoice number).
  2. Logged-in user role (never share passwords).
  3. Screenshot of the error dialog and message.
